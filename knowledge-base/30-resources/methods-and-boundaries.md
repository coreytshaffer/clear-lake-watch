# Methods And Boundaries

## Public Dashboard Boundary

The dashboard can summarize public environmental information. It should not
present itself as official public-health guidance, a regulatory advisory, or a
medical risk assessment.

## Weather Context Boundary

Weather can help explain environmental conditions and potential drivers. Weather
context should not be phrased as proof of lake-health status or bloom severity.

## Forecast Boundary

Forecasting or bloom-severity prediction is a future research direction. It
should stay separate from observed public data until there is documented model
methodology, validation, and uncertainty language.

## Note-Writing Rule

When a note makes a claim, include:

- What source supports it.
- What date or time range it covers.
- What uncertainty or limitation matters.
- Whether it belongs in public UI, methodology, or private planning.
