# Secchi Mentor-Review Handoff

**Status:** mentor-review handoff packet  
**Audience:** faculty mentor, internship mentor, technical reviewer, or qualified field-method reviewer  
**Prepared:** 2026-05-17  

This handoff packet is a short review path for the [Secchi depth / clarity mentor-review protocol](secchi-depth-clarity-mentor-review-protocol.md). It is designed to help a reviewer decide whether the draft is scientifically reasonable as a student learning protocol before any field pilot is considered.

Clear Lake Watch remains a late prototype / early MVP. It is not official public-health guidance, an official advisory, recreation guidance, emergency guidance, a validated forecast, a deployed sensor network, or an active public submission system.

## Current Decision

Keep the Secchi protocol mentor-review-only for now.

This packet does not authorize field work, create public field data, approve a QAPP, validate Secchi measurements, diagnose bloom conditions, estimate toxin risk, or support recreation-safety decisions.

## What Feedback Is Needed

The most useful feedback is practical and bounded:

- whether Secchi depth / clarity is an appropriate first mentor-reviewed student protocol;
- whether the official source anchors are sufficient or whether a better Lake County, EPA, SWAMP/CEDEN, USGS, or lab method source should be added;
- whether the draft method, metadata, QA/QC checks, and public wording limits are reasonable;
- what should be changed before any future private pilot field-note decision.

## Reviewer Questions

1. Is Secchi depth / clarity a reasonable first student-led protocol to review for Clear Lake Watch?
2. Are the Lake County QAPP, EPA volunteer lake monitoring manual, EPA lake field operations manual, and EPA volunteer QAPP guide the right initial source anchors?
3. Should another official method source be added before this draft is used for learning or mentor feedback?
4. Is the proposed unit and precision, meters to the nearest 0.1 m, appropriate for a student learning protocol?
5. Are the candidate site rules strong enough for safety, permission, access, and public-location precision?
6. Are the required metadata fields sufficient for a future private field note?
7. Should duplicate readings, a second observer, or repeat visits be required before any pilot discussion?
8. Are weather, glare, wave, recent-rain, and surface-condition notes treated with enough care?
9. Should paired photos be allowed for private QA, and what privacy or location rules should govern them?
10. Does any wording accidentally imply public-health, recreation, advisory, forecast, or official monitoring authority?
11. What exact changes are required before this could be reconsidered as a private pilot field-note protocol?
12. What should remain out of scope even after review?

## Decision Gate After Review

After feedback is received, choose one:

- keep mentor-review-only and revise the draft;
- keep mentor-review-only and archive the review notes;
- consider a separate private pilot field-note design, but only as a new bounded decision.

Do not convert this handoff directly into a field protocol or public export.

## Suggested Short Message To A Reviewer

I am building Clear Lake Watch as a student environmental data systems prototype. I drafted a Secchi depth / clarity protocol as a mentor-review-only learning artifact, using the Lake County Clear Lake QAPP and EPA volunteer/lake-monitoring guidance as source anchors. I am not treating it as approved field work or public monitoring. Could you review whether the source anchors, method steps, metadata, QA/QC checks, and public wording limits are reasonable, and what would need to change before any future private pilot discussion?
