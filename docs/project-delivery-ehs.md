# Project Delivery, EHS, And Environmental Systems Governance

**Status:** public portfolio positioning aid
**Audience:** Career Services, internship reviewers, mentors, and environmental project reviewers
**Prepared:** 2026-05-15

This page explains how project management and EHS-informed practice fit into Clear Lake Watch and the broader environmental systems portfolio. The goal is not to claim senior project-management or safety authority. The goal is to show an early-career operating model: environmental analysis, field-data workflows, project delivery, and safety/risk governance belong together.

Clear Lake Watch remains a late prototype / early MVP. It is not official public-health guidance, an official advisory, recreation guidance, emergency guidance, a validated forecast, a deployed sensor network, or a public submission system.

## Core Frame

> Environmental systems analysis + field data workflows + project delivery + safety/risk governance.

That frame presents project management and EHS as operational layers of environmental work:

- Project delivery turns ideas into scoped deliverables, review gates, issue logs, and handoff packets.
- EHS-informed practice keeps fieldwork, data publication, and public communication tied to hazard awareness and risk controls.
- Environmental systems governance keeps source provenance, public/private boundaries, uncertainty, and community consequences visible.

## How This Shows Up In Clear Lake Watch

| Portfolio layer | Clear Lake Watch evidence | Reviewer signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope and delivery | Public backlog, GitHub issues, release notes, validation script, review packets. | Can organize work into milestones and acceptance criteria. |
| Data quality and risk controls | Source-freshness validation, stale-source warnings, public/private export boundaries. | Understands that environmental data can mislead if freshness and limits are hidden. |
| GIS and site review | Site registry QA, map trust filters, unresolved marker status, dashboard anatomy guide. | Preserves spatial uncertainty instead of overclaiming map authority. |
| Public communication | Methodology page, evidence summary, Career Services packet, conservative resume language. | Can translate technical work into reviewer-ready language without overstating claims. |
| EHS-informed thinking | Data QA notices, non-advisory language, future field/microscopy review workflow. | Recognizes safety and public-risk boundaries before field deployment or public intake. |

## Safe Professional Positioning

Use:

> I am building applied competency in environmental project delivery, EHS-informed fieldwork, and risk-aware environmental systems analysis.

Use:

> My portfolio connects GIS, public environmental data, source-quality review, project documentation, and safety-conscious field workflow planning.

Avoid:

> Certified EHS manager.

Avoid:

> Safety professional.

Avoid:

> Qualified industrial hygienist.

Avoid:

> Environmental compliance expert.
