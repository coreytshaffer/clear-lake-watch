Clear Lake Watch is a late prototype / early MVP watershed intelligence dashboard for Clear Lake, California. It organizes public environmental data, GIS context, source-status metadata, site-registry QA, and methodology guardrails into a cautious situational-awareness and research-planning surface.

It is not official public-health guidance, a validated forecast, a deployed sensor network, or a monitoring authority.
